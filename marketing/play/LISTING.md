## Full description (4,000 characters)

```
Join two dots. Close a triangle. Take the yard.

Triangles – Cats vs Dogs is a cosy dots-and-lines strategy game, the old pen-and-paper classic with a furry rivalry. Take turns drawing a line between two dots. Lines can't cross, and they can't touch another dot. Close a triangle with nothing inside and it's yours. When no lines are left, whoever owns the most triangles wins.

Easy to learn in one match. Then you notice that every line you draw might hand your rival a triangle, and the real game begins.

PICK YOUR SIDE

Play for Team Cat or Team Dog. Your rivals always come from the other side of the fence: Rex the beagle, Daisy, Bruno and the rest of the pack, or Patch, Mittens, Marmalade and the cats. Switch teams any time.

A NEIGHBOURHOOD TO WIN

Fifty levels across five zones, each with its own twist:
• The Backyard – learn the ropes
• The Park – treats that make a triangle worth three
• Rainy Lane – puddles no line can cross
• Fence Row – fences already standing, and half-built triangles
• Night Garden – fireflies that light up new dots mid-match

Every fifth level is a puzzle, every tenth a boss. Beat a zone's boss and two new friends join you, each with a small power of their own. After the Night Garden the neighbourhood starts again, harder, with every twist mixed in.

RIVALS THAT GROW WITH YOU

The first matches are gentle, so you can learn by winning. Your rivals get a little sharper every level, and ease off if you lose a few in a row. Stuck? Ask for a hint, or undo your last line.

MORE TO DO EVERY DAY

• The Daily Yard: a new special board every day, with a reward that grows with your streak
• Stars on every level, and replays to collect them all
• 14 achievements to earn
• Daily tasks, a Rewards Box, and Bell Quest: win seven in a row and share the prize
• Yard themes in the Shop: picnic blanket, sandy beach, snow day, starry sky
• Two players on one phone: pass it across the table and settle it properly

FREE, WITH FAIR ADS

Watch an ad if you want to double a win's coins or take back three moves after a loss. Past level 10, a short ad also plays every few minutes, always announced first with an Ad break and never in the middle of a move. No in-app purchases, no account to make.

If you grew up playing dots and boxes on the back of a notebook, or you just have strong opinions about cats and dogs, the yard is waiting.
```

About 2,400 characters. The search phrases (dots-and-lines, dots and boxes, strategy, pen-and-paper) appear once or twice each where they read naturally. Repeating them more counts as spam on Play and can get the listing demoted.
